Refund Policy

Last updated: draft, pending final owner/legal review.

Owner/legal review required: the wording below describes our actual current refund mechanics. Final legally-reviewed wording, and any jurisdiction-specific statutory rights, should be confirmed before launch.

How refunds work today

There is no automatic, self-service monetary refund button in the product. Refund requests are handled manually by our support team - contact us via Support and we will assess your request.

What we check

  • A payment that fails at Stripe grants zero credits - there is nothing to refund.
  • A Checkout session you cancel or abandon grants zero credits - there is nothing to refund.
  • A successful, verified payment grants its purchased (and any promotional bonus) credits exactly once.

Approved monetary refunds

If we approve a monetary refund, it is processed back to your original payment method through Stripe by our admin team.

Refunds and your credit balance

A monetary refund does not automatically reduce your credit balance, and never forces it negative - even if you have already spent some or all of the credits from that purchase. Any credit-balance correction related to a refund is made as a separate, explicit, individually audited admin adjustment, not an automatic side effect of the refund itself.

No fixed refund window

We do not currently offer a blanket guarantee such as "all sales are final" or a fixed 7/14/30-day money-back guarantee. Each request is assessed on its own facts.

Statutory rights

Nothing in this policy overrides any statutory consumer-protection or cancellation rights you may be entitled to under the law that applies to you. If a statutory right gives you a stronger entitlement than described above, the statutory right applies.

How to request a refund

Contact the configured support address with your account email and purchase details (pack purchased, approximate date). We aim to respond promptly.
