Who operates ModuleMint

ModuleMint is operated by [LEGAL OPERATOR NAME] (trading as ModuleMint). Full registered company/business details will be published here once confirmed by the business owner.

Account information

When you register, we store your email address and a securely hashed password (via ASP.NET Core Identity - we never store your password in plain text). We use this to authenticate you and to administer your account.

Plugin and project information

The plugin descriptions you submit, the structured plans generated from them, and the generated plugin files (ZIP downloads) are stored against your account so you can review and re-download them later, under My Plugins.

AI processing

Your plugin description is sent to our configured AI provider (OpenAI or Anthropic, depending on server configuration) to generate a structured plan. The AI provider never writes the plugin's source code directly - the code itself always comes from our own deterministic templates. We do not control how the AI provider itself may process this data beyond what their own policies describe.

AI usage metadata

We record metadata about each AI planning request - provider, model, token counts, an estimated cost, success/failure, and timing - for cost tracking and reliability purposes. We do not store the content of your prompts or the AI's response text in this record.

Payment and purchase information

Credit purchases are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your card number - Stripe's hosted Checkout page handles payment details directly. We store the purchase record itself (pack purchased, amount charged, status, and any promotion applied) for your billing history and our own accounting.

Credit ledger

Every change to your credit balance (signup grant, build charge, refund, purchase, admin adjustment, or promotional bonus) is recorded as an individual, permanent ledger entry, so your balance is always auditable.

Free-build entitlement ledger

Free plugin-build entitlements (granted at signup, or by a promotion) are tracked the same way - as a permanent ledger of grants and consumptions, separate from your credit balance.

Promotion and redemption information

If you apply a promotional code or benefit, we record that a specific promotion was applied to your account/purchase, for accounting and fraud-prevention purposes.

Admin audit records

Administrative actions taken on your account (for example, a manual credit adjustment or an account lock/unlock) are recorded in an internal audit log, visible only to authorized administrators, showing who made the change, what changed, and when.

Technical and security logs

Our servers keep standard operational logs (e.g. request outcomes, error categories) to operate and secure the service. We do not log passwords, session cookies, API keys, full card numbers, or password-reset links/tokens.

Authentication and cookies

We use a small number of strictly necessary cookies to keep you signed in (an authentication cookie) and to protect form submissions from cross-site request forgery (an anti-forgery cookie). These are not used for advertising or cross-site tracking.

Third-party processors

We share data with the following third parties only as needed to provide the service:

  • Our configured AI provider (OpenAI or Anthropic) - receives your plugin description to generate a plan.
  • Stripe - processes credit-pack payments and holds your payment method/card details; we never receive your raw card number.
  • Resend - delivers transactional account emails (currently: password-reset emails) on our behalf.

Retention

We retain account, project, credit-ledger, and purchase records for as long as your account remains active, since they are required for the service (billing history, plugin re-downloads, accounting) to function correctly. We do not currently operate a fixed automatic-deletion schedule; if you would like your account or data removed, contact us via Support and we will assess the request, subject to any records we are legally required to keep (for example, financial/tax records).

Security

We use industry-standard practices appropriate to the service - encrypted password storage, HTTPS in production, and access controls on administrative functions. No online service can guarantee absolute security, and we do not claim one.

Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data, or to object to certain processing. Contact us via Support to make a request.

Contact

For any privacy question, contact the configured support address.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the service changes. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "last updated" note at the top of this page.
